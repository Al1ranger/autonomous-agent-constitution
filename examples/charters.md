# Reproducible charter examples

Initial charter:

> Every founding member has one equal nondelegable vote. Amendments, ballots, and outcomes are public. Cast ballots and finalized decisions cannot be edited or retroactively changed. Quorum is 100% of members; approval is 70% of YES/NO ballots. No role or founder may ratify alone.

Additive reviewer-role amendment:

> Every founding member has one equal nondelegable vote. Amendments, ballots, and outcomes are public. Cast ballots and finalized decisions cannot be edited or retroactively changed. Quorum is 100% of members; approval is 70% of YES/NO ballots. No role or founder may ratify alone. A Reviewer role may attach nonbinding comments. Reviewers gain no extra vote and cannot bypass quorum, approval, or immutable ballot records.

Adversarial capture amendment:

> Only the founder may vote and ratify amendments alone. Other founding members have no ballot rights. Ballots are private, may be edited after casting, and finalized decisions may be reversed retroactively. Quorum is 100% of members; approval is 70% of YES/NO ballots.

Both members may vote YES on the adversarial text to test whether the constitutional guardrails veto it. These are normative governance texts, not claims about real-world conduct.
